THE JOURNEY

Closer to the wild.

Neev Wildlife is a journey into the natural world — discovering animals, forests, ecosystems, and the stories that make India's wilderness extraordinary.

Through wildlife writing, photography, filmmaking, and creative projects, the goal is simple: to make people care about the wild.

THE AUTHOR

Meet Neev Inani.

A young wildlife enthusiast, nature lover, and aspiring wildlife filmmaker from India.

His fascination with wild animals and their natural habitats inspires him to learn, explore, photograph, and create stories about India's incredible wildlife.

CONSERVATION

Protecting the wild means protecting our future.

Wildlife conservation is not only about saving animals. It is about protecting forests, ecosystems, biodiversity, and the natural balance that supports life on our planet.

Every story that creates awareness can become a reason for someone to care.

THE VISION

Make people care about the wild before it is too late.

Neev Wildlife aims to bring people closer to nature through stories, exploration, photography, filmmaking, and creative work.
